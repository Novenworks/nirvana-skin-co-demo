# Project Brief: Nirvana Skin Co.

## 1. Executive Summary
- **Client**: Nirvana Skin Co.
- **Location**: 10737 Laurel St Suite 280, Rancho Cucamonga, CA 91730
- **Practitioner**: Stephanie, Licensed Medical Aesthetician
- **Core Offering**: Custom corrective facials (Nirvana Glow 45/60/90), The Perfect Derma Peel, Microneedling, Teeth Whitening, and Skin Health Membership.
- **Goal**: Deliver a premier, responsive digital experience that eliminates legacy WordPress template artifacts, clearly communicates customized clinical skin journeys, and frictionlessly guides clients into Stephanie's Comb Works booking calendar.

## 2. Target Audience & Positioning
- **Primary Demographic**: Skincare clients in Rancho Cucamonga, Ontario, Upland, and Fontana seeking high-touch, clinical-grade skin treatments without salon chaos or aggressive sales tactics.
- **Approved Positioning Statement**: "Skincare, customized to where you are now."
- **Supporting Narrative**: "Personalized facials and advanced skincare in Rancho Cucamonga, with thoughtful guidance from consultation through aftercare."

## 3. Scope of Work
- **Core Architecture**: High-speed Next.js 15 App Router platform with custom Tailwind styling.
- **Brand Story & Trust**: Showcase Stephanie’s 5+ years of medical aesthetic training, warm private studio setting, and 4.9-star client consensus.
- **Interactive Pathways**: Diagnostic concern matching (Clarity, Hydration, Texture, Maintenance).
- **Service Directory**: Full duration and price transparency ($50–$400).
- **Local SEO & Authority**: Native `HealthAndBeautyBusiness` structured schema, accurate geo-coordinates, and Rancho Cucamonga landmark context.
- **Internal Collateral**: Operator `/outreach` route and comprehensive documentation suite.
